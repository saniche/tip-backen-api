Local Truck Driver
West End Supply
Hamilton, Ontario
Full-time
Salary: CAD $25-$30 per hour

## About the role

West End Supply is seeking a safety-minded driver for local deliveries to business customers. The driver will follow assigned routes and report delivery or vehicle issues.

## Responsibilities

- Complete scheduled local deliveries.
- Perform routine pre-trip vehicle checks.
- Confirm orders and maintain delivery records.
- Load and unload goods using safe work practices.

## Requirements

- Valid Ontario Class A driver's licence.
- Safe driving habits and dependable attendance.
- Previous local delivery experience is an asset.
