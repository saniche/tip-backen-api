Warehouse Clerk
Capital Supply Warehouse
Ottawa, Ontario
Full-time
Salary: CAD $19-$23 per hour

## About the role

Capital Supply Warehouse is hiring a dependable clerk to help with receiving, organizing, and preparing stock. Training is provided for warehouse procedures.

## Responsibilities

- Receive deliveries and compare items with packing lists.
- Organize stock and assist with basic inventory counts.
- Pack and label orders for pickup or delivery.
- Keep storage and work areas clean and orderly.

## Requirements

- Able to follow instructions and work safely.
- Reliable attendance and attention to detail.
- Warehouse experience is helpful but not required.
