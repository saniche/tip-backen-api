Seamstress
Atelier du Coin
Montreal, Quebec
Part-time
Salary: CAD $20-$25 per hour

## About the role

Atelier du Coin is looking for a careful seamstress to assist with basic alterations and clothing repairs. Some tasks are completed with guidance from an experienced team member.

## Responsibilities

- Hem garments and complete simple clothing repairs.
- Measure and mark fabric before sewing.
- Use sewing machines and keep tools organized.
- Maintain a clean work area and check completed items.

## Requirements

- Basic sewing-machine experience.
- Careful measuring and attention to detail.
- Alterations experience is an asset.
