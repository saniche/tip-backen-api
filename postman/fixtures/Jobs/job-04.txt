Sales Associate
Harbour Home Goods
Kingston, Ontario
Part-time
Salary: CAD $17-$21 per hour

## About the role

Harbour Home Goods is looking for a friendly sales associate to help customers find household products and keep the store organized. On-the-job training is available.

## Responsibilities

- Greet customers and help them locate products.
- Refill shelves and keep displays tidy.
- Process routine purchases using the point-of-sale system.
- Ask a supervisor for help with questions or issues as needed.

## Requirements

- Friendly communication and a helpful attitude.
- Reliable and comfortable working as part of a team.
- Retail experience is an asset but not required.
