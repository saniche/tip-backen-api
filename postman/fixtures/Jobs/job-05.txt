Customer Service Assistant
Parc Community Services
Gatineau, Quebec
Full-time
Salary: CAD $19-$23 per hour

## About the role

Parc Community Services is hiring an assistant to welcome visitors, answer routine questions, and support front desk activities.

## Responsibilities

- Welcome visitors and direct them to appropriate services.
- Answer routine questions in person and by phone.
- Help schedule room bookings and maintain basic records.
- Keep the front desk organized and share complex requests with a supervisor.

## Requirements

- Clear and polite communication skills.
- Organized, patient, and comfortable helping the public.
- French required; English is an asset.
- Previous customer service experience is helpful.
