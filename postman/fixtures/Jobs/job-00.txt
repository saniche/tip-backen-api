Job Description:

NO SOLICITATION REQUIRED!

As a Food Advisor at East Angus Grocery, you will have the incredible opportunity to present our company, our unique grocery alternative, and our exceptionally high-quality products directly to customers during appointments provided by East Angus Grocery.

We are proud to offer an innovative alternative to traditional grocery shopping. We provide our customers with a personalized and convenient shopping experience by supplying a carefully selected range of high-quality products. As a Food Advisor, you will be the face of this alternative and will be able to enthusiastically share its unique benefits.

During the appointments provided by East Angus Grocery, you will have the opportunity to meet clients directly and present our company, our values, and our food offerings. Highlight the exceptional quality of our products, their local origin, and the health and well-being benefits they provide. Also, explain how our alternative to traditional grocery shopping simplifies our clients' lives by offering convenience and practicality.

We understand the importance of supporting our Food Advisors at East Angus Grocery. In addition to the appointments provided, we offer you a dedicated team that will provide continuous support. Take advantage of extensive training to develop your communication and sales skills, as well as resources to assist you daily. We value your professional growth and provide you with the means to succeed.

Who we are:

We are a young, family-owned business rapidly expanding in Quebec. Our mission is to optimally meet the food needs of households in our beautiful province by offering a unique service and competitive market offering. By encouraging local producers, we aim to develop a business here.

The benefits of working at East Angus Grocery:

Dynamic team: You will have the opportunity to interact with a dynamic team that wants to see you grow within the company and develop your full potential.
Discounts on East Angus Grocery products: Enjoy advantageous discounts on our products.
Contribution to the development of a local business: Join us in our mission to develop a local business and support local producers.
Professional development: At East Angus Grocery, you can evolve at your own pace in a healthy and understanding environment. We will encourage you to develop your skills and achieve your goals.
If you are a charismatic, resourceful person comfortable in public, you are the person we are looking for. Join our team now and discover a rewarding professional experience, don't miss this exceptional opportunity!
